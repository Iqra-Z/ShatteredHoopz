The Concept

Style Doesn't Stop at the Sideline

Shattered Hoopz is a design concept exploring where basketball culture and streetwear meet.

Built From the Court Up

Basketball Has Always Been a Look

Basketball has always been more than a sport — it's a walk, a fit, a way of carrying yourself. Shattered Hoopz takes cues from the game itself: bold numbers, clean lines, and color that means something, then translates them into pieces meant for everyday life, not just game day.

Shop the Collection

Three Threads

Court. Street. Culture.

Court

From playground runs to arena lights, basketball gives people a language of movement and expression. That energy is the starting point for every design decision here.

Street

Streetwear takes sport's boldness and makes it wearable anywhere — the block, the classroom, the subway. Shattered Hoopz sits right at that intersection.

Culture

No two fans wear the game the same way. The goal is pieces flexible enough to be styled as an individual statement, not a uniform.

Designed for Movement

Cut for How You Actually Move

Every piece starts with range of motion, not just aesthetics. Mesh panels, reinforced seams, and considered proportions mean the collection holds up whether you're running a full-court game or just running errands.

What Guides Us

Design Principles

Movement First

Every cut is considered for range of motion first, aesthetics second — never the other way around.

Community

Basketball is played in groups, on shared courts. The collection is designed to feel like it belongs to a scene, not just an individual.

Restraint

Small, considered drops instead of constant noise — quality over volume, every time.
